          ABOUT

          Why Locked-In

          Locked-In is a private focus timer for deep work. Pick one goal, protect the time, track your breaks, and keep a personal record of showing up — history, insights, notes, and to-dos included.

          No account. No cloud. Everything stays in this browser until you export a backup. Customize the look, install it like an app, and build a habit you can actually see.

          Your sessions never leave your device. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing uploaded.
